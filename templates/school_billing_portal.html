Renewal instructions

  1. Ask the platform administrator to issue a renewal invoice for your school.
  2. Pay using the payment method supplied by the platform administrator.
  3. Keep the payment reference/transaction ID.
  4. The platform administrator or verified payment gateway confirms the payment.
  5. After confirmation, your subscription dates and plan status are updated automatically.

Security: Never send your school password, platform credentials, Paystack secret key or Flutterwave secret hash as proof of payment.
